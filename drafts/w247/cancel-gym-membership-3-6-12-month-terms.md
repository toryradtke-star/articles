## What happens when a Workout 24/7 membership term ends?

When a Workout 24/7 membership term ends, you can renew or you can stop. That's true at both the Wells and Osakis gyms.

A set term works differently from a month-to-month plan. You agree to a length up front, and in exchange you know the monthly rate and the end date before you sign. Once the last month of your term is done, you've met your commitment and the decision is yours again.

- **To keep going:** you can renew. The rates are listed on each town page, so you'll know the price ahead of time. Call your town's gym with any questions.
- **To stop:** that's fine. Your agreement has run its course.
- **Not sure yet:** give your town's gym a call before your last month so you know where you stand.

If your life changes mid-term, call Workout 24/7 in Wells or Osakis; a freeze or transfer is usually possible. The gym would rather work it out than have you disappear.

## How do the 3, 6, and 12-month terms work?

Workout 24/7 offers three term lengths, 3, 6, or 12 months, and the monthly price is the same for all three at both the Wells and Osakis gyms. You pick a membership type and a term length, sign the agreement, and pay monthly until the term ends.

### Monthly rates (same in Wells and Osakis)

- **Single:** $39.95 a month, plus a one-time $49 joining fee
- **Couple or 2-person:** $74.95 a month, plus a one-time $68 joining fee
- **Family:** $99.95 a month, plus a one-time $68 joining fee

The joining fee is paid once and covers your key. If you lose the key later, a replacement is $20. Active duty, National Guard, and veterans get 10% off the monthly rate at both gyms. Bring your ID the first time and the discount stays on your monthly rate.

### What a full term adds up to

Since the monthly rate doesn't change with term length, the total is just the monthly price times the number of months, plus the joining fee once. For a single membership:

- **3 months:** $119.85 in monthly payments + $49 joining fee = $168.85
- **6 months:** $239.70 in monthly payments + $49 joining fee = $288.70
- **12 months:** $479.40 in monthly payments + $49 joining fee = $528.40

For a couple, a 12-month term comes to $899.40 in monthly payments plus the $68 joining fee. For a family, it's $1,199.40 plus $68. Full rates for each town are on the [Wells gym page](/wells) and the [Osakis gym page](/osakis).

## Which term length should you pick?

Because Workout 24/7 charges the same monthly rate for 3, 6, and 12-month terms, the best term is the one you're sure you'll use the whole way through. There's no discount for committing longer, so there's no reason to stretch past what fits your plans.

- **Pick 3 months** if you're new to working out, trying the gym out, or not sure how your schedule will shake out. It's the smallest commitment and costs the same per month.
- **Pick 6 months** if you know you'll be around and want to get through a season, like the stretch of winter when walking outside isn't much fun.
- **Pick 12 months** if the gym is already part of your routine and you'd rather not think about renewing for a year.

If you're on the fence, the short term is the low-risk choice. You can always renew when it's up. It also helps to know what you're signing up for.
