At a keycard gym with no staff, classes, or trainers, you let yourself in with your own key whenever you want, use the weights and cardio machines on your own, and leave when you're done. Nobody checks you in, nobody leads a class, and nobody tells you what to do next. You bring the plan; the gym provides the room and the equipment.

Workout 24/7 runs two gyms like this, one in Wells, MN and one in Osakis, MN. Both are open 24 hours with keycard access. This article covers what that actually looks like day to day, so you can decide whether it fits how you like to work out.

## What is it like to work out at a gym with no staff?

Working out at an unstaffed gym is quiet, simple, and entirely on your own terms. At Workout 24/7 in Wells and Osakis, there are no staffed hours, so there's no front desk to walk past and no one to sign in with. You unlock the door with your key, do your workout, and head home.

Most people who like this setup like it for the same few reasons:

- **Your schedule, not the gym's.** Early before work, late after a shift, or the middle of the night. The gym is open 24 hours.
- **No sales pitch.** There's no one waiting to upsell you on training packages or supplements.
- **Less of a crowd.** Without class times pulling everyone in at once, you can usually spread out.
- **No pressure.** You can try a new lift, take your time, or keep to yourself.

The trade-off is that nobody is there to coach you, spot you, or answer a question on the spot. You need to know roughly what you want to do when you walk in, or be willing to figure it out.

## How do you get in without anyone at the front desk?

You get into Workout 24/7 with your own key, which is covered by the one-time joining fee when you sign up. The joining fee is $49 for a single membership and $68 for a couple or family membership.

### Signing up and getting your key

1. Pick your town (Wells or Osakis) and your term (3, 6, or 12 months).
2. Pay the joining fee and sign the membership agreement. You can do this from your phone.
3. Stop in at the gym. Your key will be waiting, and you'll get it handed to you along with a walk-through of the place.

For that first visit, bring shoes, a water bottle, a towel, and your ID. The paperwork gets set up and you leave with your key. You can start the whole process on the [how to join page](/join).

### If you lose your key

A replacement key at Workout 24/7 costs $20. Keep it somewhere you won't misplace it, since your key is how you get in the door.

## What equipment is there if there are no classes or trainers?

Both Workout 24/7 gyms, in Wells and in Osakis, have the same basic setup: free weights, strength machines, and cardio equipment, plus open space to stretch. Here's what's on the floor at each location:

- Free weights, benches, and racks
- Strength machines and cable stations
- Treadmills, bikes, and ellipticals
- Open floor for stretching and warm-ups
- Restrooms

That covers most of what people actually do at a gym: lifting, cardio, and mobility work. What it doesn't include is group fitness. There are no spin classes, no yoga sessions on a schedule, and no personal training at either location. If you like to follow a routine, you can bring one on your phone, use a workout app, or follow a written program you've used before.

## Who is a keycard gym without staff a good fit for?

An unstaffed keycard gym like Workout 24/7 is a good fit for people who already know what they want to do and mostly need a place and the equipment to do it. It's a poor fit for people who want someone else to run the workout.

### It usually works well if you:

- Work odd hours, shifts, or long days and can't make it to a gym during normal business hours
- Have lifted or trained before and follow your own program
- Want to walk on a treadmill or ride a bike in the winter without driving to a bigger town
- Prefer to work out without being watched or chatted up
- Want a straightforward monthly price without extras bundled in

### It may not be the right fit if you:

- Need a class or a coach to stay motivated
- Want hands-on instruction on how to use equipment safely
- Are looking for a trainer to build a program for you

If you're new to lifting, that doesn't rule you out. Plenty of people start on the cardio machines and the strength machines, which are easier to learn on your own, and work up from there. If you lift heavy by yourself, choose weights you can control without a spotter, and it's never a bad idea to let someone at home know when you're heading to the gym late at night.

## What happens if something breaks or you have a question?

If something breaks at Workout 24/7, you tell the owners and it gets fixed. That's the whole maintenance policy, and it's the same at both gyms.

Since there's no one on site to report a problem to, you call or reach out directly:

- **Wells** (149 S Broadway, Wells, MN 56097): (507) 553-3211
- **Osakis** (815 1st Ave E NE, Osakis, MN 56360): (507) 993-8249

The same goes for questions about your membership. If your situation changes partway through your term, call. A freeze or a transfer is usually possible, and the owners would rather work something out with you than have you just stop showing up. You can also find both numbers and a contact form on the [contact page](/contact).

## How much does an unstaffed keycard gym cost?

Workout 24/7 costs $39.95 a month for a single membership, $74.95 a month for a couple or 2-person membership, and $99.95 a month for a family membership. The rates are the same in Wells and Osakis, and the monthly price is the same whether you choose a 3, 6, or 12-month term.

- **Single:** $39.95/month, $49 one-time joining fee
- **Couple or 2-person:** $74.95/month, $68 one-time joining fee
- **Family:** $99.95/month, $68 one-time joining fee
- **Replacement key:** $20

Monthly dues are paid by automatic withdrawal from a checking account or a card. Cash or check works for the joining fee and key.

Active duty, National Guard, and veterans get a 10% military discount at both gyms. Bring your ID on your first visit and the discount stays on your monthly rate.

One thing to know up front: the membership isn't cancel-anytime. It's a signed agreement for a 3, 6, or 12-month term and runs to the end of that term. When it's up, you can renew or stop. To see the full details for your town, check the [Wells gym page](/wells) or the [Osakis gym page](/osakis).

## Frequently asked questions

### Is anyone working at Workout 24/7?
No. Workout 24/7 in Wells and Osakis has no staffed hours. Members let themselves in with their own key. When you first join, you pick up your key and get shown around.

### Does Workout 24/7 offer classes or personal training?
No. Neither the Wells nor the Osakis gym offers classes or personal training. Both have free weights, strength machines, cable stations, cardio equipment, and open floor space for working out on your own.

### What if I lose my gym key?
A replacement key costs $20 at either Workout 24/7 location. Call the gym in your town to get a new one.

### Can I cancel my membership anytime?
No. Memberships are a signed agreement for 3, 6, or 12 months and run to the end of the term. If something changes mid-term, call the gym; a freeze or transfer is usually possible.

### Can I sign up without going in first?
Yes. You can pick your town and term, pay the joining fee, and sign the agreement from your phone. Your key will be waiting at the gym when you stop in.
